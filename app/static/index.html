Everything above works without this. If you add your own API key, a short excerpt of the top problems (up to 5 groups, each sample cut to about 1,200 characters, never the whole log) is sent to the provider you choose, and that provider processes it under its own terms. Your key is used for this one request, is not saved by this site, and is not kept in your browser. Do not use this for logs you are not allowed to share with that provider.

What this tool does and does not do

  • It groups repeated errors by pattern. It does not understand your code.
  • Python, Java, Node.js, Go and C/C++ crashes are read in detail. Other languages are grouped by their message lines. JSON-lines and CSV logs are read field by field.
  • Only the most recent 50,000 lines are analyzed. Files up to 5 MB, pasted text up to 1 MB.
  • Your log is analyzed in memory and not saved. Avoid pasting secrets such as passwords or tokens.
